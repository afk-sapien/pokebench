# Starter pilot with the revised subscription harness

Two user-authorized live runs used the ChatGPT subscription through Codex CLI
0.147.0, harness `codex-cli-cooperative-v2`, and benchmark
`red-v0.4-experimental`. Each model started from the same verified bedroom save,
with no Pokemon. The goal was to obtain a starter from Professor Oak.

Both runs used low reasoning effort, the structured track, no name-label table,
and the original 160 by 144 screenshot. Limits were 30,000 reported tokens,
12 model calls, 96 controller actions, 36,000 emulator frames, 600 frames per
action, eight actions per decision, and 300 wall seconds. There were no retries,
manual gameplay interventions, API charges, or redeemed reset credits.

| Model | Outcome | Calls | Actions | Input tokens | Output tokens | Total tokens | Wall seconds |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| gpt-5.6-sol | Token reservation stop | 5 | 8 | 28,150 | 603 | 28,753 | 30.30 |
| gpt-5.6-luna | Token reservation stop | 6 | 10 | 24,785 | 896 | 25,681 | 30.93 |

Neither left the bedroom or acquired a Pokemon. Sol ended at map 38, x=1, y=6.
It repeatedly described the bed on the left as a staircase. Luna ended at map 38,
x=2, y=6 after trying directions around the initial position. Its final notes
still placed the stairs or exit upward and leftward.

The controllers advanced 264 frames for Sol and 221 for Luna, equivalent to
4.42 and 3.70 game seconds. Both chose short batches despite the eight-action
ceiling. Model latency does not advance the paused emulator. Wall duration is
therefore not the same as gameplay progress.

All 11 calls had complete usage accounting. Total reported usage was 54,434
tokens. Sol averaged 5,630 input tokens per decision and Luna averaged 4,130.83.
The reservation stopped both before requesting a likely unaffordable next
response. Neither exceeded 30,000 tokens. This observed behavior does not turn
the estimate into a guaranteed provider-side token cap.

These input counts are below the older control runs, but the checkpoint and
observation payload also changed. This is not an isolated measurement of the
prompt change's effect. Remaining overhead is still substantial.

The account's shared weekly meter moved from 23% to 24% during this work. The
rounded meter includes other account activity and does not isolate this pilot's
subscription cost.

## Verification and provenance

Both complete action traces passed deterministic replay, including screenshots,
private scoring evidence, frames, score, and trace hashes. The successful
controller reference completed the starter task in 67 actions and 9,621 frames.
The model traces do not contain that reference solution.

Local runs:

- `runs/starter-v04-sol-01`
- `runs/starter-v04-luna-01`

The scenario is `scenarios/starter-repro-v04`, prepared from
`fixtures/opening-starter-v1.json`. Manifests pin the model, prompt, source,
core, ROM, state, image, limits, CLI, and reasoning settings. Individual
`verification.json` files record replay checks. Sanitized outcomes and artifact
hashes are frozen in `paper/analysis/inputs/benchmark.json`.

No success or comparative model ranking is established by these two trials.
Before spending more, investigate screen interpretation and remaining Codex
context overhead. Any changed image presentation, labels, prompt, or budget
requires a separate recorded comparison group.
