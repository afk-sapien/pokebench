# Terra starter trial with a 500,000-token budget

The user requested a Terra comparison after the Luna trial. This run used
`gpt-5.6-terra`, low reasoning effort, and the ChatGPT subscription through Codex
CLI 0.147.0. Its manifest matches the extended Luna run apart from the model and
start time. Checkpoint, runtime source, instructions, structured observations,
original 160 by 144 screenshot, lack of name labels, and all limits match.

## Outcome

Terra reached Pallet Town but did not obtain a starter. Score was 0/1. Recorded
post-action locations included the bedroom (map 38), the ground floor (map 37),
and Pallet Town (map 0). It first reached town on decision 63, action 3, at frame
4,104. It later returned inside and finished on the ground floor with an empty
party. No action ended in Oak's lab.

Its notes repeatedly misidentified the current location. Replayed screenshots
for decisions 69 and 83 show the ground floor while its notes describe Pallet
Town. However, decisions 64 and 76 show the house fading out even though RAM
already reports map 0. The earlier interpretation that those particular images
showed an outdoor scene was too strong. This correction concerns interpretation,
not the recorded task outcome. No gameplay hints or interventions were supplied.

All calls have complete usage accounting. The threshold is enforced between

Secondary limits were 150 calls, 1,200 actions, 360,000 emulator frames, and
3,600 wall seconds. Actions allowed at most 600 frames and batches at most eight
actions. None of these limits ended the trial. The protocol remained
`red-v0.4-experimental` with harness `codex-cli-cooperative-v2`.

## Comparison and verification

Luna used 499,402 tokens, 112 decisions, and 244 actions without leaving the
bedroom. Terra reached additional locations with the matched budget, but both
failed the starter task. This single trial per model does not establish a
reliable ranking or isolate the effect of sampling variability.

Terra's complete action trace passed deterministic replay, including frames,
screenshots, evaluator evidence, score, and trace hashes. The trace head is
